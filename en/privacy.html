Last updated: 20 October 2026

In one sentence: Programas collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and it sends nothing to any server of mine, because there is no server of mine.

What is stored and where

The app stores on your device what it needs to work: the lessons you have done, the programs you have watched, your practice minutes, your favourites and your daily goal. That file never leaves your device.

Your code

What you write in the sandbox is stored only on your device, inside the app, and runs in the app's own interpreter, offline. It is not sent anywhere.

Purchases

The full-course purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. I receive no data about you or your payment method.

What there isn't

  • No user accounts or sign-up.
  • No ads or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, telemetry or tracking.
  • No third-party components that collect data.

Crash reports

If you have "Share With App Developers" turned on, Apple may send me anonymous technical crash reports. Apple handles them and you can turn them off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Since the app collects no personal data, it collects no data from children either.
